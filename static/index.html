A QUICK GUIDE

Make yourself at home.

From a stack of books to a library in your pocket.

01

Photograph. Queue. Repeat.

In Scan, choose one book or shelf spines. For one book, add its cover and optionally its spine and back. For a shelf photo, include 1–5 upright spines and set the number of books.

Add the photo to the queue, then move straight to the next book. The AI works through the queue one at a time. Keep BookBridge open while uploading; queued scans continue on the server.

02

Check what the AI found.

Open Scan → Review. Each book from a shelf photo gets its own result. Tap Review, correct the title and author if needed, then add it to your library. Unclear spines are best photographed individually.

Accept all ready skips individual review and saves the current AI results, including possible duplicates.

03

Everything lives in Library.

Choose Covers, Shelves or List. Shelves show book titles on spines; pinch to zoom or open Filters for the zoom buttons. Search by title, author, ISBN or shelf. Tap a book to read its details, or tap its cover photo to enlarge it. Multiple copies are grouped together.

Saved details stay read-only until you press Edit. Changes save automatically while editing. Wait for “All changes saved” or press Done before leaving. If a save fails, your edits remain open so you can retry.

04

A little more connected.

Use the library selector to switch between your own collection and shared or friends’ libraries. Friends holds connections and trades. Settings holds your profile, privacy and scan preferences.
